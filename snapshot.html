Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Wednesday, August 5, 2026

LU-481 Indianapolis, IN is running hot with 32 calls and only 150 hands on book — Google and Meta data centers pulling heavy. Ermco's got 26 spots on the Google Moorsville gig (5-10s + Saturday work), while Meade Electric is hiring 15 JWs at $53.80/hr for Meta Lebanon on brutal 5-12s Mon-Fri plus 10-hour Saturdays with 10% over-scale kicker. This is the kind of relentless schedule that thins boards fast.

LU-291 Boise, ID shows serious money if you can stomach the grind. Prime Controls has 56 opens on Micron ID1 at $45.33/hr base but adds $170/day per diem (7 days if eligible) plus $20/hr travel premium — that math works out to real coin on a PLA job. VECA's got META work at true scale $65.33/hr with incentive OT premiums, the sweet spot if you want top dollar without data center slavery hours.

LU-1105 Newark, OH is slinging premium OT: Superior Group's pulling 13 on Vantage and 11 on Conesville (4-10s + 2-8s) with all OT double time. That's the structure that makes six-figure summers happen. ESI's running $2.01 over scale day shift on Project NAB/NAH.

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI (35 calls, 201 hands) — Cupertino, Pieper, Gurtz, and MJ are all stacked on Port Washington Data Center. JW wage plus $10/hr premium, but running 50-60 hour weeks (some 60s). Wisconsin hands know this contractor pool. Drug test required.

LU-613 Atlanta, GA holds 55 calls across four contractors. Inglett & Stubbs alone has 63 spots split between QTS Fayette and Wellstar Paulding Hospital at $40/hr — comfortable scale, reasonable hours, lower cost of living. Not glamorous, but steady work.

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND (22 calls, 102 hands) is aggressive: Parsons Electric hitting 60 JWs on Polaris Forge at $44.80/hr base plus $100/day non-taxed per diem, $100–$150/day incentive (weekends bump to $150), and another $100/day if you pull ND license. That's $544.80/day walking money on a 10-hour shift. Six-10s, but the board's thin and work's real.

The move: Data centers and PLA projects are vacuuming up hands nationwide — 1083 open calls across 165 locals means boards are bleeding out. Chase the premiums (Wisconsin's $10/hr bump, Boise's travel money, Newark's double-time OT structure) or chase the grind (Indianapolis, Grand Forks) where supply's tight and desperation pays.

Parsons Electric dominates with 60 inside JW slots at $44.80/hr, non-taxed $100/day per diem, and incentive pay ranging $100–$150/day depending on day of week. Six 10-hour days, all OT premium. Hunt Electric and Northern Valley are filling air base work at the same scale with 4-10s and Friday OT premiums.

VECA's META fab work mirrors that rate; their Micron call matches. This is semiconductor infrastructure — high volume, stable duration, locked pay scales.

LU-1547 Anchorage, AK is thin on calls (47 total, only 62 hands) but tells the real story: pay scales are weak across the board — nothing above $66/hr, and most slots are non-union classification work (laborers, mechanics, groundmen). Tree work tops out at $43.55/hr. The Alaska board is hungry but offering less.

LU-583 El Paso, TX has 9 calls but solid rates. Summit and Cupertino are both at $55/hr (NM JIW license and OSHA 10 required), all OT double time. Rosendin's $40/hr base + $15 incentive is the outlier.
