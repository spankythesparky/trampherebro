Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Monday, October 5, 2026

LU-613 Atlanta, GA is sitting on 127 calls with nearly 1,000 hands available—the board's drowning in bodies. Miller Electrical and Inglett & Stubbs are running the QTS Fayetteville data center hall build with conduit and cable tray work at $42/hr. It's steady work but the depth is brutal; don't expect quick dispatch out of this one unless you land early.

LU-583 El Paso, TX is the pay-play if you've got mobility. Cupertino's Santa Teresa job hits $70/hr with a fat $1121.00 per week per diem for the 350+ mile move—that's serious money for NM State JIW licensed hands. You'll need OSHA 10 and a clean test, but the math works. Coltrane's Meta El Paso sit is $45 with a tighter $100/day PD.

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI has 55 calls chasing 301 hands on the Port Washington Data Center jobs—Cupertino and Gurtz Electric both offering JW wage plus $10/hr base wage increase on top. That's over-scale movement and they're still filling. Tight board, steady project.

LU-405 Cedar Rapids, IA is burning through the EWD Data Center call—CST's running 42 JW slots on six 10s starting 7AM with $10 over scale per diem and all OT double time. OSHA 10 required. This is the kind of work density that moves fast; 68-hour weeks are live at QTS if you want the hours.

LU-575 Portsmouth, OH just posted fresh calls. TSC's North Campus Amazon job (Jeffersonville) starts *today* with 90 hands on 4-10s; Blattner's Palomino Solar Fields is 9 JW slots at $100/day PD on 5-8s. Both starting Monday.

LU-354 Salt Lake City, UT has Cache Valley running QTS and Facebook with $46.22 base, $10–$20 over scale per diem, and solid scheduling (5-10s, some Saturdays). Smaller board but clean work.

Data center buildouts are still the gravity well—Milwaukee, Cedar Rapids, and Salt Lake are moving volume. El Paso pays the premium but requires licensed mobility.

LU-602 Amarillo, TX — 3 calls, 11 hands. HEARTLAND ELECTRIC is running 8 Inside JW in Amarillo proper at $50/hr, 6-10s. DACON's Gale and Afton work pays $53.72/hr and $40.23/hr respectively with $150/day per diem and 6-10s schedules.

LU-583 El Paso, TX — tight board but hot: B&D Industries' Santa Teresa, New Mexico project is $70/hr fire-alarm-focused work, while Mass Electric on the same site runs $55/hr all-OT-double-time. Both require NM State JIW License EE98J.
