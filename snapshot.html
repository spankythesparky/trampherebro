Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Sunday, August 16, 2026

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND is running hot right now. Parsons Electric's got 77 Inside JW calls across two separate gang calls at Polaris Forge Data Center in Harwood, all at $44.80/hr with $100/day non-taxed per diem plus $100/day incentive Mon-Fri, $150 Sat-Sun on 6-10's. Hunt Electric's pulling another 10 at Grand Forks Air Base same rate, same per diem structure, 4-10's with Friday OT. That's real money stacking on top of scale—the incentive premiums are the play here if you can stomach North Dakota winter and don't mind the grind.

LU-430 Racine, WI has Microsoft Mount Pleasant locked down tight. Four contractors (Gerdes, Pieper, Staff Electric, Kelso Burnett) are moving 53 JW hands all at $62.51/hr—flat across every call, which means the board's unified on pricing. Gerdes is the biggest move at 22 bodies; Pieper's at 13. All require Wisconsin JW or reciprocal, long sleeves, safety toes. Clean, predictable work.

LU-613 Atlanta, GA sits at 56 total calls, 315 hands deep—the biggest board on this snapshot. Inglett & Stubbs dominates with 41 JW spots at QTS Fayette on $40/hr, 4-10's and 2-8's Mon-Sat, Saturdays optional. Miller Electrical's got 20 more at the same rate pulling medium voltage. Book depth here means expect some wait, but the volume is real.

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI shows 38 calls, 189 hands. Cupertino's Port Washington Data Center job pays JW wage plus $10/hr on 58–60 hrs/week with drug test required. Pieper's service truck work carries foreman pay for drivers. Data centers are pulling serious OT across the board.

LU-683 Columbus, OH has 28 calls, 91 hands at Cologix and ECX projects—all $46/hr with 5-10's+8 and all OT paid at double rate. McGraw Kokosing and ESI are the anchors.

LU-1547 Anchorage, AK is an outlier: 54 calls but only 71 hands, dominated by non-Inside work (temp laborers, tree trimmers, telecom). Inside JW calls are buried.

Data center construction is absorbing hands faster than anything else on the board right now—every major local's best volume is QTS, Barn, Polaris Forge, or similar.

Rosendin's Meta build (44 JW) runs $40/hr base plus $15 incentive for 90% of 60/week, all OT double time. Big desert plays pulling wire.

LU-70 Forestville, MD — 8 calls, 59 hands. Didado's transmission Old Field WV job tops the board: 20 JW at $126/hr, $150/day per diem, classified operator-transmission work. LU 1245 CA/NV ops at $55.90/hr across various sites. Transmission premiums are real money here.

LU-291 Boise, ID — 17 calls, 51 hands. VECA Electric's META 3&5 data-center build runs 8 Inside JW at $65.33/hr with incentive program and additional OT premium pay (6 more at same rate). Stuart Olson and Lorneville are pulling 44, 39, and 10 journeymen pipefitters apiece on days, with another 11 apprentices running nights. The refinery and semiconductor corridor is absorbing hands fast; shutdown and fab work is where the board depth matters.
