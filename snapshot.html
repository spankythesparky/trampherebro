Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Monday, August 17, 2026

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND is running hot with 26 calls and fat incentives. Parsons Electric's Polaris Forge Data Center in Harwood is the anchor—42 Inside JW slots at $44.80/hr plus $100/day per diem (non-taxed) if you're 100+ miles out, bumping to $150 Sat/Sun. That's another $100/day if you hold an ND license. Six 10s or die trying. Hunt Electric's Grand Forks Air Base call mirrors it at 4-10s with Friday OT built in. This board is printing money for remote guys willing to grind.

LU-430 Racine, WI has 11 calls but they're all the same Microsoft Mount Pleasant job at $62.51/hr—four contractors (Gerdes, Pieper, Staff Electric, Kelso Burnett) running parallel crews. 22, 13, 10, and 8 slots respectively. Book 1 is moving. Long sleeves, safety-toe, and you need Wisconsin Inside JW or two years verifiable elsewhere. All OT paid double.

LU-583 El Paso, TX shows serious spread. Rosendin's Meta facility in El Paso runs 44 Inside JW at $40/hr with $15 incentive for 90% of 60 hours weekly—all OT doubled. But Summit Line's Project Miner in Santa Teresa, New Mexico climbs to $55/hr with $1,121/week per diem for JIWs over 350 miles out. Twenty-seven slots. Eight more at Cupertino Electric at the same rate.

LU-613 Atlanta, GA sits fat with 52 calls, all rated, top single call at $48.00/hr. But the working board is Inglett & Stubbs and Whitehead Electric at $40/hr—three calls ranging 20 to 41 Inside JW each. DataBank Fulton, QTS Fayette, Wellstar Paulding. Book 1 clearing fast.

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI has 36 calls anchored by three contractors splitting the Port Washington Data Center—Cupertino, Pieper, Gurtz, MJ Electric—all paying JW wage plus $10/hr on 50–60 hour weeks. Thirty slots on that one job. Drug test and physicals required.

Data center work is eating the board right now. Every region pushing electrical for AI compute clusters, and the per diems are real.

Trampin the IBEW Lineman Locals — Monday, August 17, 2026

LU-71 Columbus, OH is moving serious volume—46 calls stacked with work. NEW RIVER ELECT. CORP. has 40 JL slots on distribution rebuild, $125 per diem nights stayed plus $125 bonus on 10-hour days, 6-10s with CDL required. Another 9 JL distro gig same contractor in Southern Ohio, 4-10s with optional Friday and Saturday. CAPITAL ELECTRIC LINE BUILDERS has 6 JL cable splicing work in Johnstown, $100 per diem, OSHA 10 and Class A CDL hard requirements. Deep board, solid local work.

LU-583 El Paso, TX is the transmission play if you've got credentials. Summit Line's Santa Teresa, New Mexico call is $55/hr with $1,121/week per diem for those traveling 350+ miles—27 Inside JL slots, but NM State License EE98J is non-negotiable. Cupertino Electric mirrors it: 8 Inside JL at $55/hr, same per diem and licensing. Rosendin's Meta El Paso offer is deeper (44 Inside JL) but lighter at $40 base plus $15 incentive for 90% of a 60-hour week, all OT double time. Play the transmission work if you're licensed; the distro money is below-grade.

LU-70 Forestville, MD has the outlier: Didado's transmission rebuild on Old Field, WV posting $126/hr for 20 JL Operator-rated slots, $150 per diem. That's the single highest call on the board today. Distro and substation work underneath at lower rates.

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND is a data-center machine. Parsons Electric owns the Polaris Forge Data Center call—42 Inside JL at $44.8/hr, $100 non-taxed per diem Mon-Fri, $100 incentive bonuses, $150 on weekends, additional $100/day for ND license, 6-10s. Hunt Electric's Air Base call (10 Inside JL, same $44.8) runs 4-10s with 8 OT Friday. ND license bumps you another $100 daily on both—certification work is paying real money here.

LU-84 Newnan, GA has 30 calls lean and specialized. SERVICE ELECTRIC's Social Circle transmission job demands Quanta Barehand cert and trans experience for $51.8/hr, 11 on 3 off, $150 per diem. Same contractor's energize side pays $46.8/hr with Josh Harrelson dispatching (404-617-2410), 17 JL, $150 per diem.

Data-center work and transmission are eating the board right now—specialized certs and ND/NM licensing are straight-up wage multipliers.

Trampin the UA Locals — Monday, August 17, 2026

LU-290 Portland, OR is running the biggest board right now—28 calls, 46 hands needed—and it's all Intel and Charter Mechanical work in Hillsboro. High Purity Fitters, Orbital Welders, BIM Detailers, and tool install/demo across the Intel Ronler Acres campus. Book depth is tight. DSI's QTS job at Eagle Mountain is offering $10.00 on a 5-8 schedule with mixed 5-10s and single 8s. August start already rolling.

LU-208 Denver, CO has 9 calls, 41 hands. US Eng. Metalworks wants night-shift fitters in their shop with stainless experience; Harris's Project Waterfall pays per diem of $125 if you're outside 100 miles; Murphy's Coors 4-can filler demands OSHA 10, running 5-10s. Broadcom in Fort Collins (US Eng. Construction) seeks high-purity fitters with tube-bending chops. No scale listed, but these are solid industrial names.

LU-776 Lima, OH is compact at 8 calls, 27 hands, but one job shows $53.00—Graham's Cenovus maintenance gig needing 5 current-DISA pipefitters. Gem's pulling 7 combo welders for fab-shop work and 3 more fitters for the Ineos outage on 6-10s. Sidney Municipal Building (Bodie) has 2 plumber spots.

Irving Oil refinery work and semiconductor fab builds are hoovering up hands; Portland's Intel boom is real.
