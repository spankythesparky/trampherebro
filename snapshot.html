Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Friday, August 21, 2026

LU-613 Atlanta, GA
Fifty calls stacked deep with steady data center work. Whitehead, Inglett & Stubbs, and Miller Electrical all posting $40/hr cable and splicer gigs on the QTS Fayette DC—4-10s and 2-8s Mon-Sat, six-day grind but no surprises. Miller's cable splicer call bumps to $40.90/hr on a five-day rotation if you've got three years documented. Book's not drowning you here; solid entry point for traveling hands.

LU-430 Racine, WI
Microsoft Mount Pleasant is the story. Four contractors—Gerdes, Pieper, Staff Electric, and Kelso Burnett—all chasing the same $62.51/hr build. That's top-tier money for Inside JW work. Wisconsin reciprocal license or better, conduit bending skills, long sleeves mandatory. Eleven calls on the board, seventy-six hands waiting—Book 1's moving but this one pays to sit tight.

LU-583 El Paso, TX
Cupertino's Santa Teresa job in New Mexico is the outlier: $55/hr on a straight Inside JW call, $1121/week per diem for anybody 350+ miles out, NM State JIW License EE98J required. Rosendin's Meta El Paso running $40/hr base with a $15 incentive for hitting 90% of 60-hour weeks—all OT double time. Ten calls total; the Santa Teresa premium is real if you're licensed.

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND
Parsons Electric's Polaris Forge Data Center in Harwood dominates: $44.80/hr, $100/day per diem non-taxed, $100/day M-F incentive, $150 weekends, plus $100/day with ND license. Thirty-six Inside JW slots. Hunt and Northern Valley posting the same rate on Air Base work. Twenty-two calls, seventy-nine hands—Book 1 moving steady.

LU-237 Niagara Falls, NY
Lake Mariner Data Center and Amazon both paying $48.20/hr. Ferguson and O'Connell each running 7-10s day shift, capping intake at ten to fifteen bodies daily—orientation Tuesday only at O'Connell. Six calls, seventy-nine hands: tight board, good money, move fast if you're heading east.

LU-354 Salt Lake City, UT
Facebook Eagle Mountain, Rocky Mountain Power Terminal, Delta Center—multiple $46.22/hr Inside JW calls with five-day schedules and OT available. Wasatch and Rydalch throwing $100–$150/day incentive pay on ten-hour days. Twenty-one calls, seventy-one hands; book's healthy and work's distributed across solid contractors.

Data center builds and industrial maintenance are vacuuming hands nationwide—book depth is real everywhere, but money's concentrating in specialty infrastructure (power, thermal, security) where certifications and experience move you off the call sheet fast.

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND — 22 calls, 79 hands. Parsons Electric's Polaris Forge data center near Harwood is stacked: 36 Inside JW at $44.8/hr, $100/day non-taxed per diem, $150 weekends, plus $100/day ND license incentive on 6-10s.
