Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Wednesday, September 23, 2026

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI is the heavyweight here. Gurtz and Cupertino are both running Port Washington Data Center with JW wage plus $10/hr base wage increase — that's real money on top of the book rate. Thirty JWs each into what looks like a long-cycle build. Wil-Surge and Pieper round out a tight 42-call board with 193 hands on the bench. This is the play if you want scale-plus and steady work.

LU-430 Racine, WI is Microsoft Mount Pleasant, all day. Four contractors (Gerdes, Pieper, Staff Electric, Kelso Burnett) all listing the same call at $62.51/hr — that's top-tier Wisconsin inside work. Eleven calls total, seventy-six hands deep. Reciprocal cards honored. The board's small but the rate is fat.

LU-237 Niagara Falls, NY has Lake Mariner Data Center popping hard. Ferguson and O'Connell are each taking forty JWs at $48.20/hr with PD incentives available, first shift 6:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Ferguson caps new arrivals at fifteen per day, so move fast if you're in the area. Four calls, eighty-three hands — book won't last.

LU-613 Atlanta, GA sits on fifty-eight calls with 425 hands available. Miller, Inglett & Stubbs, and Whitehead are all running QTS Fayetteville data center work at $42/hr — 79, 52, and 39 JWs respectively. Cable tray, feeder pulls, hall build-out. Long cycle, multiple contractors mean steady dispatch.

LU-575 Portsmouth, OH is the emergency board. TSG alone has ninety-five JWs into Amazon North Campus starting Monday, September 28, running 4-10s plus an 8. Hanley's got four more. Two unfilled calls on Amazon South. If you can be in southern Ohio by Monday morning, there's immediate work.

LU-354 Salt Lake City, UT shows Skyline, Wasatch, Cupertino, and Rydalch all feeding Eagle Mountain / QTS at $46.22/hr, 5-day schedules with OT and per diem incentives (Wasatch running $225 per 10-hour day). Sixteen calls, seventy-seven hands — data center builds are still pulling hands hard across the belt.

Data centers remain the board's gravity well. Texas pay is soft; upper Midwest and coastal Northeast are where the scale lives.

Trampin the IBEW Lineman Locals — Wednesday, September 23, 2026

LU-71 Columbus, OH is the board heavyweight at 46 calls hunting 140 hands. New River Elec is moving volume—40 JL on distro work around Columbus with $125 per diem nights stayed plus a $125 bonus on 10-hour days, running 6-10s with a Class A CDL required. Same contractor has 9 more JL in Southern Ohio, 4-10s with optional Fri/Sat. Capital Electric wants 6 JL for cable splicing in Johnstown on the overhead side, OSHA 10 and Class A CDL hard stops.

LU-84 Newnan, GA sits at 31 calls, 135 hands, with the heat on energized work. Service Electric is running two separate calls at $56.80/hr—20 JL in Social Circle needing that Quanta barehand cert for both GTC and standard energized work, $150 per diem, 5-10s. Heart Utilities is underneath at $46.80/hr in Savannah with the same $150 per diem bump.

LU-1393 Indianapolis, IN has 39 calls chasing 63 hands. ARC American tops out at $64.66/hr on a 12.5k rebuild in Wanatah—no per diem but solid distro work. Kent Power runs distribution storm work in Valparaiso at $56.66/hr, $125 per diem. MJ Electric wants transmission tech experience on 345kv lattice towers in Fort Wayne, same $56.66/hr, $100 per diem plus room/site rent.

LU-66 Houston, TX has 21 calls, 74 hands. NHPL is moving 19 JL on overhead distro Houston-area with all OT double time and $150 per diem, Class A CDL mandatory. Same contractor has 9 more JL on transmission in Floresville bumped to $175 per diem, Class A CDL again.

LU-1249 Cicero, NY holds 27 calls on 46 hands across upstate transmission and distribution. Riggs Distler, MJ Electric, and Danella all running $100–$125 per diem with Class A/B CDL holds on 5-10s and 6-10s schedules.

Data-center work is absorbing bodies hard—Amarillo, Nashville, and Memphis are stacking inside JW calls on big AI infrastructure projects at rates well below lineman scale. Transmission and storm are still where the dollars live.

LU-140 Salt Lake City, UT is the volume play right now—18 calls, 61 hands needed.
