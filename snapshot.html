Electric Plus is staffing IU Hospital downtown (5-10s + Saturday, OSHA10 mandatory). This is bread-and-butter work with solid hours and zero screwing around.

NM State JIW License EE98J and OSHA 10 are table stakes. Fire alarm and VESDA experience moves you up the board faster.

LU-430 Racine, WI is locked on Microsoft Mount Pleasant at $62.51/hr—Gerdes, Pieper, Staff Electric, Kelso Burnett all running the same job, same rate. WI reciprocal JIW or 2+ verified years in-state. Long sleeves, safety-toe boots, and conduit bending are non-negotiable.

LU-237 Niagara Falls, NY has 7 calls but Ferguson, O'Connell, and BESCO are all loading Lake Mariner Data Center with 7/10s (6:30am–5pm), per diem live, and first-shift orientation—92 hands chasing 7 calls means Book 1 is getting thin fast.

Market trend: Data center work is pulling hands north and east; Indiana and Charlotte are absorbing bodies fastest, while Wisconsin and upstate New York are offering stability at solid scale with per diem backup.

LU-1393 Indianapolis, IN shows ARC American posting $64.66/hr on 12.5k distribution rebuilds in Wanatah—clean transmission work without per diem beating. Newkirk's Ball State T-Line rebuild pays $51.79/hr with $200/day per diem, older scale but solid per diem structure.

LU-84 Newnan, GA pairs grid experience with $46.80/hr from Inception Energy in Savannah plus $100/day per diem, statewide call. Fairway's Columbus gig onboards 8/3 for the same rate.
