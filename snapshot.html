Trampin Day in Review

Where the work is right now — top-paying locals, the biggest boards, and the projects driving demand, pulled live from union dispatch.

Trampin the IBEW Indoor Locals — Wednesday, September 23, 2026

LU-494 Milwaukee, WI is running hot. Sixty-two calls, only 287 hands on the books—that's a 4.8:1 ratio and Book 1 is moving. Gurtz Electric and Cupertino are both staffing the Port Washington Data Center at JW wage plus $10/hr base wage increase, and that's real money on top of scale. Three separate Cupertino calls for 30 JWs each means they're hammering. Wil-Surge Electric has 16 more inside work locally. This is the tightest board in the briefing.

LU-354 Salt Lake City, UT has 32 calls chasing 154 hands—clean 4.8:1 ratio, all priced at $46.22/hr. Skyline Electric and Wasatch Electric are both running META and QTS data center builds at Eagle Mountain. Wasatch is throwing $225 per diem incentives on each 10-hour day worked Monday through Friday, plus another $225 on Saturday OT—that's real walking money if you're five-day or six-day schedule. Five-day schedule with OT available. Book 1 is definitely working.

LU-575 Portsmouth, OH is the dark horse: only 5 calls but 117 hands on the books means selective hiring. TSG has 94 JW slots on Amazon North Campus in Jeffersonville starting Monday, September 28, running 4-10s plus an 8. Blattner's got 14 more on Palomino Solar Fields in Hillsboro at 5-8s. These aren't data center gigs—this is heavy industrial ramp-up work with immediate start dates.

LU-20 Dallas, TX and LU-60 San Antonio, TX are both Texas mixed boards: Dallas has 52 calls at $40.65/hr on Morley Moss Electric's STACK Lancaster project (4-10s schedule), plus CW/CE work. San Antonio's 29 calls lean apprentice and CW, night shift at Big State ($6p–$4:30a all hours premium-paid), but pay data is sparse.

Data centers and utility-scale solar are eating the board. Milwaukee and Salt Lake are where the JW work density is highest; Portsmouth is the biggest immediate-hire shock.

Trampin the IBEW Lineman Locals — Wednesday, September 23, 2026

LU-84 Newnan, GA is sitting fat with 14 calls and 76 hands on the board. HEART UTILITIES is running 20 Journeyman spots in Savannah on 5-10s at $46.8/hr, plus $150 per diem. FAITH ELECTRIC has 10 more around College Park/Columbus pulling $49.8/hr — that's $3 above JL scale — with hotels covered and per diem at $150. If you've got your CDL and Subtech ticket, SERVICE ELECTRIC and INCEPTION ENERGY SOLUTIONS both have statewide calls at $44.46/hr with $150 per diem. This board moves quick.

LU-1426 Grand Forks, ND is humming with data center and transmission work. Apex Line Contractors has two lineman spots at $58.5/hr on 6-12s with $100/day per diem plus $100 incentive Mon-Fri and $150 Saturdays. Parsons Electric is pulling 15 Inside JW at the Polaris Forge Data Center on 6-10s, $44.8/hr base with non-taxed $100/day per diem and the same incentive structure. Northern Valley has Air Base and fire alarm work at the same rate — all tight schedules, all hands moving.

LU-70 Forestville, MD has 8 calls tight with transmission and substation work. Thayer's running 6 Journeymen in Shenandoah Valley needing CDL and clean record; Miller Bros has four more local substation spots at $126/hr, $125 per diem. That's specialty pay for certified work.

LU-1393 Indianapolis, IN sits at 14 calls with solid rebuild and tower work. P&G Power's three-man Goshen crew on 69kv with 12kv underbuild pulls $67.18/hr, $100 per diem. ARC American's Wanatah 580 rebuild is five spots at $64.66/hr — no per diem but 12.5k line work.

LU-583 El Paso, TX punches above weight: B & D Industries has six fire alarm/VESDA spots in Santa Teresa, New Mexico at $70/hr needing NM JIW license.

Data center builds and critical infrastructure work are eating the board — Northeast and Southwest transmission projects are where the real rates live right now.
